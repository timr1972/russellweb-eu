I just picked buttercup up from the local tyre garage who spent 5 hours setting up the tracking on all 4 wheels; camber and toe in front and rear as well as checking ride height.  What a difference it makes from setting it up by eye! As you can see in the image above the rear wheels had 13.9 degrees of toe in, the front wasn't much better and the camber was all over the place.

![image](<http://www.russellweb.eu/wp-content/uploads/wpid-img_20140716_203658.jpg?w=400>)

Unfortunately they did mange to destroy one of the cycle wings in the process, but agreed to reduce the bill by the cost of a replacement wing. The wings I originally purchased are no longer available so I'm going to gave to order a new pair and hope they get here by Friday so I can get them on in time for the show in Sunday.
