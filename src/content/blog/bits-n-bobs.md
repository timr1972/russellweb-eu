The rear lights and harness along with a few other things, all turned up last week. Yesterday I took the time to solder on the wires for the rear lamps and to seal them off. They only need to be fitted to the body and have aplug on the end of the cables to connect to the loom.

The harness has also been fitted in and checked for size. I can get in and buckle in and still reach all of the controls, except one. Although the battery isolator doesn't really have to be used while driving so I guess we don't have to worry about that!
