I've been thinking about the wiring loom and how I was going to stash it all away. Because of the abundance of wires, I was concerned about the shear volume, and so I've decided to make the loom to fit exactly the car, with all, or a majority of the cables being cut and soldered to the correct length. This is going to be extremely labour intensive, but will be worth it's while in the long run, it will also make it easier to apply heat reflective tape and protection to the chassis rails and cable runs.

Tomorrow I'll do a trial run with a few wires and see how it goes...
