As I posted last night, I've made the decision to make the loom to size, rather than wrapping excess wiring inside the loom tape.  The biggest benefit is it that I can place components where I want them, and remove excess cables.

Each of the wires in main head loom has been cut to length and soldered then covered with heat shrink.
