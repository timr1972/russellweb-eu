The steering wheel has had another revision, this time with a NeoPixel Ring, an Arduino Flora and a HC-05 Bluetooth module, all combined into a 4 cm circular unit, less than 15mm deep, perfectly sized for the steering wheel centre.

[![20140216-133719.jpg](<http://www.russellweb.eu/wp-content/uploads/20140216-133719.jpg>)](<http://www.russellweb.eu/wp-content/uploads/20140216-133719.jpg>)This is the Flora, the NeoPixel ring and Bluetooth unit are all sized to fit within the diameter of the Flora. I have designed the centre cover that will show system status, lights, fog lights, beam and indicator status. Just trying to find a supplier who will make a reverse laminated one off for me. This photo is showing the paper based mockup, but the final article should look the same.[![wpid-20140212_122845.jpg](<http://www.russellweb.eu/wp-content/uploads/wpid-20140212_122845-300x225.jpg>)](<http://www.russellweb.eu/wp-content/uploads/wpid-20140212_122845.jpg>)
