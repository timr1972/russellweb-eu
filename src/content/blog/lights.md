I'm not happy about the rear lights, there I've said it.  The sidelights specifically,  as although they are LED they are not very bright. This was first noticed a few years ago driving around the Paris ring in heavy rain on my way to Le Man's.  Apparently the rear lights were completely invisible as they were shrouded in rain spray and were not bright enough to penetrate the rain.

As you can see from the photo,  I think they could be better...
